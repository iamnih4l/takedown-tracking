Auditing Methodology & Statutory Legal Standards

Mathematical formulations, statistical right-censoring proofs, and statutory IT Rules legal frameworks.

2026 IT Rules Statutory Deadlines

Under Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules (Gazette Notification 269993, effective 20 February 2026):
• Rule 3(1)(d): 3.0 hours for specified court orders or authorized government intimations.
• Rule 3(2)(b): 2.0 hours for qualifying grievance complaints (CSAM, non-consensual imagery, impersonation).
• Section 79(3)(b) Data Assistance: 72.0 hours for lawful subscriber / IP disclosure requests.

Right-Censored Survival Percentiles

Standard percentile calculations that drop unresolved requests create severe survivorship bias. Our engine applies survival analysis: unresolved overdue requests are ranked after finite durations. If the median or 90th percentile rank touches an unresolved case, the metric is right-censored (> measurement window), preventing speed inflation.

Measurement Cutoff (as_of) & Maturity

To prevent unfair penalties against newly dispatched orders, requests dispatched close to the as_of cutoff whose statutory deadline has not yet elapsed are classified as immature and excluded from compliance-miss denominators.

k-Anonymity (k=10) Privacy Protection

To protect crime victims from targeted re-identification or correlation attacks, all platform and category denominators are evaluated independently. Cohorts with fewer than 10 cases are suppressed from public outputs. Small counts of incomplete requests and rejections are redacted to [Protected < k].
